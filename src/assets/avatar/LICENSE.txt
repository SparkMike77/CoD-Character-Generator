Avatar models and textures in this folder are derived from
"Universal Base Characters" by Quaternius - https://quaternius.com
Licensed CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication):
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Generated by scripts/prepare-avatar-assets.js (textures greyscaled for
tinting, downscaled, and packed into .glb). Regenerate from the original
download rather than editing these files.

The Condition poses (js/avatar-poses.js) are single frames of "Universal
Animation Library" by Quaternius, also CC0 1.0, extracted by
scripts/extract-poses.js.

body_mannequin.glb (the "Other" body) is the Mannequin from the same
Universal Animation Library, rig and mesh only, by
scripts/extract-mannequin.js.
